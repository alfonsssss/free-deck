Free Deck – Privacy & Terms

Last updated: 16 November 2026

What we store

Free Deck has no accounts and asks for no email or password. To make the game work we store:

  • On your device (browser storage): your table, language, sound setting, the name you choose and a random code that lets you delete games you made.
  • On our game server, only when you play with others or publish a game: the name you choose, the games you publish in the Community, how often games are played, reports of inappropriate games and, for ranked chess, your rating linked to the random code above.

We don't sell or share this data and don't use it for advertising. Ads are shown by CrazyGames under their privacy policy.

Games made by players

Games in the Community are made by players. Names, descriptions and rules are filtered for bad words and links, and a game is hidden when several players report it. Don't publish anything offensive or anything you don't have the right to share.

Removing your data

You can delete games you published from the start page. Clearing your browser's site data removes everything stored on your device. Ratings and other server data are anonymous apart from the name you chose; they are removed on request through the CrazyGames developer contact for this game.

Terms

Free Deck is free to play and provided as it is. Chips and points have no money value and can't be bought or exchanged. Be kind to other players.
